# RUH Project

> "Reserve your place before you die." / "Ölmeden önce yerinizi ayırtın."

Welcome to the RUH Project. This initiative aims to automatically detect the host body a soul will transfer to after death via energy signature locking and GPS tracking. 

## Project Phases
* **Phase 1 (Idea, R&D & Pre-Registration):** Clients fill out their personal details, declare inheritance choices, and pay a pre-registration fee to fund the legal and technological infrastructure.
* **Phase 2 (Implementation & Activation):** Once the technology and international legal frameworks are live, Phase 1 clients will have priority access to the service.

## Core Mechanics
- Clients **cannot** choose their next body. 
- An energy signature is locked into the user's body before death.
- The next host is located using this signature's GPS properties.
- Pre-arranged legal contracts guarantee the transfer of specified wealth and assets to the new identity.
